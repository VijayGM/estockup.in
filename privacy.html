Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SocialMedia AI Videos ("the Application") handles information when users interact with the application and its supported third-party integrations..

1. Application Purpose

SocialMedia AI Videos is an AI-assisted application designed to support content creation workflows, video preparation, and authorized publishing to supported social media platforms.

2. Information Accessed

The Application may request access to third-party services only when such access is explicitly authorized by the user and is required to provide requested functionality.

Depending on enabled features, this may include authorized access to Google services such as YouTube.

3. Google User Data

When a user authorizes access to Google services, the Application accesses only the information and permissions necessary to provide the functionality requested by the user.

Google user data is not sold to third parties.

The Application does not use Google user data for advertising purposes.

Google user data is not transferred to third parties except where required to provide the requested application functionality, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and integrity of the Application.

4. YouTube Integration

Where authorized by the user, the Application may use YouTube API services to support authorized YouTube content management and publishing workflows.

The user controls authorization of their YouTube account and may revoke access through their Google account permissions.

5. Local Data Processing

The Application is currently designed primarily for local use. Generated content, workflow information, and application data are generally processed and stored on the user's local computer.

The Application does not currently operate a centralized cloud database for the purpose of storing users' personal Google account data.

6. Data Sharing

The Application does not sell personal information or Google user data.

Information is shared only when necessary to perform functionality explicitly requested by the user, such as communicating with an authorized third-party platform API.

7. Third-Party Services

The Application may integrate with third-party services, including Google and YouTube, and may support additional social media platforms in future versions.

Use of those services may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of the respective third-party providers.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally processed application information and authorized access credentials.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Application evolves or introduces new integrations and functionality. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding SocialMedia AI Videos, please contact the application administrator through the official project communication channels.
