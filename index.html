SocialMedia AI Videos

AI-powered content creation and social media publishing automation

A technology initiative by eStockUp Venture

About the Application

SocialMedia AI Videos is a technology application designed to support AI-assisted content creation workflows, video preparation, and authorized publishing to supported social media platforms.

The application is currently being developed with support for YouTube publishing workflows and may support additional platforms, including Instagram and other social media services, in the future.

Application Functionality

  • AI-assisted content creation workflows
  • Video content preparation and management
  • Social media content automation
  • Authorized publishing to supported platforms
  • Integration with third-party APIs where explicitly authorized

Google and YouTube Integration

Where enabled by the user, SocialMedia AI Videos may connect to Google services, including YouTube, through Google's authorized authentication mechanisms. Access is requested only when required to provide the functionality explicitly initiated by the user.

Users retain control of their connected accounts and authorization.

Data Processing

The application is currently designed primarily for local processing. Application data and generated content are processed and stored on the user's local computer unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise.

Future Development

The application is under active development. Future versions may introduce additional integrations, automation capabilities, and technical tools. Any future data handling or third-party integration will be subject to applicable authorization and privacy requirements.
